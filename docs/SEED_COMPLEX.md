# Demo seed complex (MVP §2)

Use this layout for seed data. Altitudes are examples; every building is also a weather point.

| Building | Altitude | What happens there |
|----------|----------|--------------------|
| Valley station | 1,050 m | Parking, bus/train stop, ticket desk, rental shop, lower cable car |
| Main hotel | 1,200 m | Rooms, reception, main restaurant, sauna, thermal bath, spa, game room, gym |
| Chalets | 1,250 m | Family chalets, booked like rooms |
| Mid station | 1,800 m | Self-service restaurant, ski school meeting point, chairlift start |
| Mountain hut | 2,100 m | Dorm beds sold one by one, simple meals, guided hike start |
| Summit station | 2,600 m | Panorama restaurant, viewing platform, top of cable car |

Seed script is a Milestone 5 / handover deliverable, but design models so this seed is natural from day one.
